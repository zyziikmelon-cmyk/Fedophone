2. What Fedophone is, in short

Fedophone is a chat and call app for two people in a private room. Messages, voice messages, stickers and calls go directly between you and your friend. There is no server that stores or reads your conversations.

3. What is stored where

  • On your device only: your name, avatar, banner, status, settings, chat history, pins and voice messages. They live in your browser's local storage or in the Windows app's files and never leave your device except to your friend in the room. You can delete a chat in Settings → Chats or clear everything by clearing site data.
  • Account (optional): if you create one, the server keeps your account name, a salted password hash (the password itself is never stored), and your profile so you can sign in on another device. No e-mail address is required.

4. Services that technically see your connection

To let two devices find each other, third-party services receive your IP address and technical connection data, like any server. The content of the introduction messages is encrypted with a key derived from the secret room word.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting (Pages), account and relay passwords (Workers), call relay (TURN) when no direct path exists.
  • GitHub, Inc. — a second copy of the website (GitHub Pages) and the download of app updates.
  • Public message brokers (EMQX, HiveMQ, Mosquitto) and ntfy.sh — carry encrypted introduction and offline-delivery messages for a short time (about 12 hours).
  • STUN servers — only tell your device its public address.

5. The Windows app

If you allow it, the Windows app looks at the list of running programs only to recognise a known game and shares the game name, icon and play time (and, for Dota 2, CS2 and Roblox, the match state or place) with your friend in the room. This is not sent to us. It checks for updates by downloading a small public file from GitHub or Cloudflare.
